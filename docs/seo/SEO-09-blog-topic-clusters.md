# SEO-09 — Blog topic clusters

Status: In Progress

## Goal

Build a small, useful Cape Town wellness content library that supports the site's real services and location pages without mass-producing thin or invented articles.

## Publishing rules

1. Only show an article as published when its route exists and contains substantive content.
2. Do not invent publication dates, review claims, customer stories, therapist credentials, clinical benefits, or pricing facts.
3. Each article should answer one clear user question and link naturally to the most relevant service, location, and booking page.
4. Avoid exact-match keyword repetition. Write for customers first.
5. Publish one article at a time, validate its metadata/canonical/internal links, then add it to the blog hub and sitemap.
